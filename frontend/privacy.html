Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Coast Connect Kenya collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website, marketplace, booking, ordering and related services.

Last updated 9 August 2026

We aim to handle personal information responsibly, transparently and only for legitimate platform purposes.

Introduction

Coast Connect Kenya operates a digital marketplace that helps customers discover restaurants, local service providers and related services across Coastal Kenya.

This Privacy Policy applies when you access or use Coast Connect Kenya, create an account, place an order, make a booking, register as a provider or restaurant owner, contact support, or otherwise interact with the platform.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us when creating or managing an account or using platform features.

Account information

This may include your full name, email address, phone number, password credentials in securely hashed form, account role, verification status and account status.

Provider information

Service providers may provide profile information such as biography, experience, service area, availability, profile image, services offered and pricing information.

Restaurant information

Restaurant owners may provide restaurant names, contact information, addresses, operating hours, menu information, delivery information, pricing, images and other business information required to operate on the marketplace.

Booking and order information

When you make a booking or order, we may collect information required to process that transaction, including service details, delivery or service addresses, dates, times, instructions, order items and status information.

Support information

If you contact Coast Connect Kenya, we may collect your name, email address, enquiry type, message and information relating to your support request.

2. How we use your information

We may use personal information to operate, maintain and improve Coast Connect Kenya and to provide the services requested by users.

This may include using information to:

  • create and manage user accounts;
  • authenticate users and protect account access;
  • process restaurant orders and service bookings;
  • connect customers with providers and restaurants;
  • provide customer and business support;
  • review provider and restaurant applications;
  • detect misuse, fraud or security threats;
  • maintain transaction, order and booking records;
  • provide notifications and service updates;
  • understand platform usage and improve user experience;
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Location information

Coast Connect Kenya may use location information to help users discover nearby restaurants, service providers, delivery areas and other location-based services.

Depending on the feature being used, location information may include a manually entered address, selected area, town, latitude and longitude, or device-provided location information where permission has been granted.

Users can control device location permissions through their browser or device settings.

4. Payments

Coast Connect Kenya may integrate with third-party payment providers to process payments for orders, bookings or other platform services.

Payment providers may collect and process information required to complete transactions. Coast Connect Kenya does not require users to provide sensitive payment credentials directly to unauthorized parties.

Transaction references, payment status and related payment records may be stored where necessary for reconciliation, support, security and recordkeeping.

5. How information may be shared

Coast Connect Kenya does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to provide platform services, including with:

  • restaurants processing customer orders;
  • providers fulfilling service bookings;
  • payment service providers;
  • infrastructure, hosting and technical service providers;
  • authorities where disclosure is legally required;
  • professional advisers where necessary for legal, compliance or security purposes.

We aim to share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Data security

Coast Connect Kenya uses technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Security measures may include password hashing, authentication controls, role-based authorization, secure API access, security headers, request rate limiting, database protections and restricted administrative access.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their passwords and account access information.

7. Data retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, enforce agreements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Certain records, including order, booking, payment and support records, may need to be retained after an account becomes inactive where there is a legitimate operational or legal reason.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights relating to their personal information, including the ability to request:

  • access to personal information held about them;
  • correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • deletion of information where legally permitted;
  • restriction or objection to certain processing;
  • information about how personal information is being used.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or recordkeeping purposes.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

Coast Connect Kenya may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support authentication, maintain user sessions, remember preferences and improve platform functionality.

Some browser-based functionality may not operate correctly if these technologies are disabled.

10. Third-party services

The platform may contain integrations or links to third-party services. Those third parties may operate under their own privacy policies and data protection practices.

Users should review the privacy practices of third-party services they choose to use.

11. Children's privacy

Coast Connect Kenya is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental or guardian consent is legally required.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected contrary to applicable legal requirements, reasonable steps may be taken to remove or appropriately handle that information.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Coast Connect Kenya may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in platform functionality, legal requirements or business practices.

The updated date displayed at the top of this page will indicate when material revisions were last made.

13. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your account, or how your information is handled, you can contact Coast Connect Kenya through our support page.
