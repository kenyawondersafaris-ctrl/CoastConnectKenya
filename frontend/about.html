About Coast Connect Kenya

Making Coastal Kenya easier to explore, book and experience

Coast Connect Kenya connects customers, visitors, restaurants and trusted local professionals through one platform designed around convenience, transparency and trust.

Built for the coast Local discovery with real utility

From finding a restaurant to booking househelp, cleaners, drivers or other professionals, Coast Connect helps people find trusted options where they are staying.

Our purpose

One trusted place for local needs

Finding reliable services in a new area can be difficult. Restaurant information may be scattered, service providers may be hard to verify, and customers may not know who to trust.

Coast Connect Kenya brings these experiences together by helping customers discover restaurants, compare information, book professional services and follow their activity through structured digital workflows.

The platform is focused on Coastal Kenya, where tourism, hospitality, local businesses and everyday household needs often overlap.
